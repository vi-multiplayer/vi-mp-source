# Community Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, useful space for people interested in independent multiplayer technology and game development.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of differing experience and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Share technical claims with sources where practical, and label uncertainty clearly.
- Respect project boundaries, intellectual property, privacy, and security.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, impersonation, or repeated disruption of technical discussion.
- Uploading or soliciting leaked, proprietary, or otherwise unauthorized material.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove violating content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers using GitHub's contact options. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
